About CryptoVault

Professional cloud mining infrastructure since 2012.

Company Story

CryptoVault was established in Malaysia in 2012 as a focused Bitcoin mining operation with a small fleet of early ASIC systems. As blockchain adoption accelerated, we expanded mining farms across multiple regions and transitioned to a global cloud mining model. Today, our platform runs enterprise-grade infrastructure backed by renewable-energy mining facilities, resilient networking, and strict operational controls to provide a secure cloud mining ecosystem for users worldwide.
